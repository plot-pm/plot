Talk: "Harness the Beast: Leading When Agents Do the Work"

Abstract

AI agents can now plan, write, and ship software faster than any team we've ever run. The hard part isn't the speed. It's knowing whether the result can be trusted, and who is accountable when it can't.

At Quatico, we rebuilt our own delivery model around agentic development. This talk shares what we learned on that journey, including where it failed. The main lesson is that the leverage is not in the model. It's in the harness around it: the guardrails, feedback loops, acceptance criteria, and human checkpoints that turn raw capability into dependable outcomes.

Participants will leave with a practical view of what leaders must design differently when agents do the work. That means redefining decision rights, moving from inspecting output to calibrating systems, and making quality assurance a leadership responsibility rather than a technical afterthought.

Bio

Jan Wloka is CEO of Quatico Solutions AG, a Swiss software engineering company focused on agentic development. After research years in the US at IBM Research and Rutgers University, he moved to Zurich to join the IBM Rational Zurich Research Lab and return to hands-on software engineering. He joined Quatico's leadership in 2014 as CTO and Partner, became co-owner in 2016, and took over as CEO in 2026. In recent years he has put AI agents into real software delivery, building the orchestration, context engineering, and quality harnesses that let teams trust what agents produce. He now helps Swiss organisations move AI from experiment to dependable value.
