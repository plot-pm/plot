## 3. The tally fix

- **The move double-counts on two paths.** (read) The reading happens only for a complete tick (`registryd-main.ts:2421`). A thrown tick `continue`s at `:2410` and an incomplete tick skips the reading. With the reset moved after the reading, those ticks' calls roll into the next reading, and `boardSharePerHour(intervalMs, calls)` (`cadence.ts:72`) divides them by one interval. `mine=` then shows a spike that the record does not show. The plan must name the reset for these paths, or divide by the time since the previous reading.
- **`mine=` and the record do not move by the same amount, so the acceptance fails as written.** (read) `minePerHour = (3_600_000 / intervalMs) × calls` with `TICK_INTERVAL_MS = 60_000` (`registryd.ts:80`). The real tick period is about 90 s (60 s pause plus the tick). One merged call per tick reads `mine=60/hr` against about 40 record lines per hour. Removing 2 calls per tick moves the record by about 80/h and `mine=` by 120/hr. `tally` also counts memo hits (`registryd-main.test.ts:1747–1768` asserts two increments for one host call), so `mine=` counts questions, not processes. Either restate the acceptance as "`mine=` moves by `calls × 3600 / interval`", or compute `mine=` over the measured time between readings.
- **Other readers.** (read) `minePerHour` has one reader, the tick line (`registryd.ts:587–590`). No cadence decision reads it (`registryd-main.ts:2420` says so, and `pause` reads only `args.intervalMs`). The tests that assert `mine=120/hr` (`registryd-tick.test.ts:1104`, `:1150`) build the report directly and do not depend on the reset. Two pieces of prose become false and must change in the slice: the `HostTally` TSDoc (`registryd-main.ts:358–364`, "sets it to zero at the top of every tick. It carries nothing into the next tick") and the `checksWaitCache` comment at `:2367` ("CLEARED HERE, beside `tally.calls`").
- **The step still hides calls after the fix.** (read) The release port (`releaseClaimOf` → `gatherReadingsAndRelease`) asks `prState` and `prMerged` outside `tally`, and the fresh-agent step shares it (`registryd-main.ts:2147–2165`). The operator decision says `mine=` reports every call the step makes. The slice must count the release's two calls too, or the plan must state that it does not.
