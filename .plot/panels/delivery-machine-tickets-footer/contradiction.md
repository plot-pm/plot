# Contradiction lens: delivery-machine-tickets-footer

Position: amend

The plan matches the design's Machine, Tickets and Footer sections and the 2026-11-10 host-freshness ruling in wording. It disagrees with HEAD, with a domain decision and with CLAUDE.md in the points below. None makes it unbuildable.

## Findings

- **misleads** — The Changelog and slice 3 say the plan "adds a footer". A footer exists at HEAD: `AgentList.tsx:1577-1620` prints "<n> branches across <n> plans · scanned Ns ago · next in Ns · PR data Ns ago · next in Ns", with the comment "This IS the view-status line, and it stays here". The new parts are only coloured dots, minutes wording and "desks not placed · details". The `<!-- builds: -->` marker and the Changelog should say "reworks" and name what is new.
- **misleads** — Open Question 1 ("which reading gives the time of the next PR read, and does one exist while no fleetd runs?") is answerable now. `schema.ts:4575` carries `prNextInSeconds` (nullable, default null), `AgentList.tsx:869-872` already derives `prNext`, and `schema.ts:4560` says the client falls back when it is absent. The open part is only the no-fleetd case, which the plan should state as such.
- **misleads** — Open Question 2 ("does Plan it › call the existing `idea` controller?") is answerable now. `AgentList.tsx:1535-1545` renders `IssueRowView` with an `idea` prop for each unplaced ticket, and CLAUDE.md lists `idea` among the controller endpoints. The plan should settle it, and name the ticket-as-source input as the real open point.
- **blocks (domain decision)** — The healthy line shows "load 2.1 of 10 cores" and the dot turns amber or red; "Left to the Builder" sets thresholds "within readings the machine already takes". `packages/domain/src/entities/machine.ts:39-50,121` says load average is "context only, never the verdict" and "never named", and the verdict is `headroomFor(spawnCostMs)` (`clear`/`tight`/`starved`). The plan must say that the dot derives from `Headroom` and that load and cores are printed as context. It must not invite a load threshold.
- **misleads** — "Readings … the board and fleetd already take" is not true for three of the four figures on the healthy line. `rg` of `packages/board/src` finds no `headroom`, `loadAverage` or CI-queue field in the payload schema; `schema.ts` has no "GitHub 4,210 / 5,000" remaining figure either (only the budget spender counts near `:4600`). The Machine entity holds load and cores in the domain, but the board does not carry them. The plan names no gap, which breaks its own rule ("where a reading does not exist, the plan names the gap") and the design's non-goal of no new host reads.
- **misleads** — The "Tickets" section is not new. `AgentList.tsx:1499-1506` renders "Tickets and plan runs", holding verify and spec runs; the 2026-11-08 ruling (`every-work-item-and-agent-shows.md:77`) says "verify runs sit under Tickets and plan runs". Sibling plan 4 (`delivery-fleet-rows-per-agent.md:92`) asks whether those runs move into Fleet. Plan 6 neither renames the block nor says what happens to those runs, so two plans may claim one section.
- **misleads** — Ordering against plan 2. Plan 2's slice 1 removes ticket rows from You (`delivery-you-blocks-or-feeds.md:74`) and its Open Questions 63 and 93 ask where the count links until the Tickets section ships. Plan 6 waits only on plan 1, so it can ship after plan 2 and leave the reader's tickets visible nowhere. Plan 6 should name this (a `waits:` or an ordering note), because the design says "Each ships alone".
- **misleads** — "Rebuild the board artifact (`pnpm build:board`) with each slice" contradicts CLAUDE.md: a PR must carry no generated bundle (`check-no-bundle-diff.sh`), and the rebuild is a local test only. Plan 1 lists the same defect as its last Open Question (cosmetic there); plan 6 does not.
- **misleads** — The Motivation links `docs/stories/delivery-tab-reads-at-a-glance/STORY-delivery-tab-reads-at-a-glance.md` and Status `Story:` names the slug; `ls docs/stories/delivery-tab-reads-at-a-glance` fails at HEAD. Plan 1 records the same gap; plan 6 does not.
- **cosmetic** — The 2026-11-10 ruling ("a host-fed reading never borrows the git scan's freshness") applies to the new GitHub-budget figure on the healthy line. No Done When asserts it, and the existing `HostAnswer` (`unasked`/`unreachable`, `host-notes.ts:218`) is the vocabulary a healthy line with no host answer must use. The plan says the rulings "stand" but does not say how the one-line form shows them.
- **cosmetic** — Terminology holds: Machine, Worker and Slice are used correctly, and plan 6 agrees with plans 1 and 2 on Tickets being unscoped and on `feature/delivery-section-header`. No other contradiction found.
