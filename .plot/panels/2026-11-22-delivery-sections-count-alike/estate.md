Change Motivation item 2 to: "Both read `not-started` in the count. The facts behind both holds are on the board's rows (`startability: needs-brief`, `blockedBy`), and fleetd names them as queue holds only in `.plot/logs/fleetd.log`."

I ran `git grep` for state files under `.plot/state/`: no file carries `HeldSlice`. `registryd-main.ts:4385-4391` writes the holds to the log only, and its own comment says "THE HELD SLICES ARE NAMED HERE AND NOWHERE ELSE". `no-brief`, `waits`, `slice-unnamed` and `not-claimable` are verdicts re-derivable from git.
