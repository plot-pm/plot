## What must change

New findings (the amendment added these claims or they were missed before):

1. **Slice 3 › `runBounded` breaks the stop path that #1084 fixed, and the plan does not name it.** `plot-dispatch.sh:1849-1867` stops an agent with `kill -TERM -- -<pgid>`, because on 2026-09-30 a single-pid signal left `claude` and its children alive under pid 1 (#1084). The comment states the invariant: "that group holds one wrapper with its monitors, its loop and its `claude`". The shell loop keeps the prompt in that group: it uses no `setsid` or `set -m` (`command grep -nE 'setsid|set -m' plot-worker-loop.sh` finds none) and kills the tree with `_kill_tree` (`:2028-2037`). The plan's `runBounded` starts the prompt `detached: true` (`run-script.ts:58-66`), so the prompt leads a new group that the stop signal does not reach. On SIGTERM the loop's handler can forward the signal. On SIGKILL, or a crash, no handler runs and a `claude` of about 743 MB RSS (this session's process, `ps -o rss`) keeps running and spending tokens with no loop. That is the #1084 orphan again, and slice 6's failure kind 4 counts only a loop that outlives its desk, not a prompt that outlives its loop. Slice 3 must either keep the prompt in the agent's group and kill its tree, or name the `--stop` path and #1084, add a test that `--stop` ends `claude` under `js`, and count a prompt outliving its loop as a loop-caused failure in slice 6.
2. **Slice 4 › Baseline: the named source is deleted by the reaper.** The plan counts the shell loop's failures "over the 7 days before it merges, from `.plot-worker.log` lines". `plot-reap.sh:144-147` states that this log "lives INSIDE the tree, so it goes when the tree does". On this machine `find .worktrees -maxdepth 1 -mindepth 1 -type d | wc -l` gives 1 desk and `ls .worktrees/*/.plot-worker.log | wc -l` gives 1 log, dated Oct 4 20:28, and `find .worktrees -maxdepth 2 -name .plot-worker.ending.json | wc -l` gives 0. The oldest dispatcher log is Oct 2 04:02 (`ls -tr .worktrees/*.log | head -1`). So a 7-day backward count cannot be taken, and the issues filed are a filtered sample. The plan must say how the baseline is collected forward (for example, slice 1 or slice 4 copies each ending and log line to a file outside the desk) and start that collection 7 days before the window it compares.
3. **Costs › CI: the e2e reason is mis-measured, and the expensive leg has no time or ceiling.** The plan keeps e2e on `shell` "because `validate` already takes 19 min 24 s". That total is true (run 37208059832, 14:18:43 to 14:38:07), but the E2E step is the cheap part: it takes 40, 43, 42 and 45 s on runs 37229680946, 37208059832, 37207651316 and 37207646658. The Helper contract step takes 408, 481, 500 and 502 s on the same runs against `timeout-minutes: 12` (720 s, `ci.yml:316`). `workerloop.test.mjs` alone takes 141 s (plan), and `ci.yml` records `dispatch.test.mjs` at 6 m 05 s, which is one of the 10 BuildMonitor files. A second pass of the loop files inside that step would pass the 720 s ceiling. The plan must say whether the `js` leg is its own job or part of the contract step, give its expected minutes, and give its ceiling. The e2e decision then needs its real reason, or the e2e files can run in the `js` leg for about 45 s.
4. **Slices 1 and 4 › Shell ratchet: the offsets are smaller than the additions the slices describe.** `check-shell-lines.sh` refuses any net growth in non-comment lines and has no override. Slice 1 adds a holding-work exit after a `ran` prompt, a remote-tip reading and a containment reading per poll, and a log arm for a moved branch. The shell loop fetches the remote once per wait (`head_is_pushed`, `:1734`), not per poll (`wait_for_checks`, `:1777-1782`), so a tip that moves during the wait needs a fetch inside the poll loop too. That is about 10 to 12 lines against 7 named. Slice 4 names no line. A slice that the gate refuses as written costs a correction round at PR time. Name the lines in each slice (all of `desk_hold_reason`, 20 lines, has one caller at `:2997` and can fold into that log line), or state the count the slice must find.
5. **Slice 4 › Coverage: the board has no coverage tool.** `packages/board/package.json` has no `@vitest/coverage-v8` (only `packages/domain/package.json:28` has it), `packages/board/vitest.config.ts` has no `coverage` block, and CI runs the board's unit tests without `--coverage` (`ci.yml:1319`). The threshold needs a new dev dependency and a CI step that runs coverage for the board. The slice should name both and the step's time, since the board's unit tests now take 26 s (run 37208059832, "Board tests").
