## Findings

1. **An empty You, Work items or Fleet section shows the clear ✓ while items or agents could not be read.** The table at plan:54-62 makes You and Work items clear on `section.count` 0, and Fleet clear on `up` with 0 agents. `placeDelivery` puts an item or agent whose reading lacks a fact into `unplaced` with reason `unread` (`packages/domain/src/rules/delivery-placement.ts:417,456,473`), and the view carries them as `view.notPlaced` (`packages/board/src/app/lib/agent-rows/delivery-view.ts:629`). Such an item is in no section, so its section can read 0 and answer "Nothing waits on you." while the reading is missing. That is the spec's anti-goal, *"Could not read" that looks like "empty"*. The spec forbids the clear form in exactly this case for the header (spec line 91: the groups "show their numbers with an amber "! N not read" clause and never their clear form"), and plan 4 builds that (`2026-11-20-delivery-tab-has-one-header.md:52,108`). With this plan as written, the header says "! 2 not read" and the open You section below it says "✓ Nothing waits on you". OPENED. Change to the plan: add `view.notPlaced` by subject to the inputs of the You, Work items and Fleet rows of the table. Where the count is above 0, the kind is unread and the sentence names the count and jumps to `unplaced-details` through the reachability rule. An item counts for both You and Work items, and an agent counts for Fleet, as in plan 4. Add one unit test cell per section for this case.

2. **The Tickets hidden count is not the count Mine hides, and its button is not the Mine setter.** Plan:67 takes the Tickets count from `ticketsSection`'s `hidden` input, and plan:66 labels the count "hidden by Mine" and gives the frame a button that calls the Mine chip's or scope toggle's setter. That input is `fleet.waiting?.hidden` (`packages/board/src/app/components/AgentList.tsx:1797`), the count of open tickets that the server-side waiting read holds back because they are not assigned to the reader (`packages/domain/src/rules/tickets-section.ts:22-23`; `hiddenSentence`, `packages/domain/src/rules/waiting-tickets.ts:256`, "open tickets are not assigned to you"). Mine is a separate filter on top: `ticketsForReader(tickets, reader, source, mode)` (`AgentList.tsx:864-866`, with the comment at `:855-863`). The `hidden` count exists with Mine off, and the tickets that `ticketsForReader` removes are not in it. The control that reveals `hidden` is *show all* (`setShowEveryTicket`, `AgentList.tsx:1799`), not Mine. Also, the section already renders a sentence for `hidden` (`hiddenSentence` prop, `TicketsSection.tsx:27,87`, from `waiting-view.ts:92`), and the plan does not say how the frame's sentence and that one relate. OPENED. Change to the plan: for Tickets, take the Mine-hidden count as the tickets passed to `ticketsForReader` minus the tickets it returns, labelled "hidden by Mine" with the Mine setter. Name `hidden` as a second, separate count that says "not assigned to you" and reveals through *show all*, and state that the frame reuses `hiddenSentence` for it rather than writing a second sentence.

3. **Three more tests read `[data-group-toggle]` as a section toggle.** Moving sections to `data-section-toggle` (plan:47) changes what these find. `mine-filter.browser.test.ts:114` opens every `[data-group-toggle]` so that a fold never hides a row the filter test counts (its comment at `:105-112` names that purpose); after the change it opens no section, so a folded section (Not placed by default, or any stored fold) defeats it. `row-withholds.browser.test.ts:275` measures the section caret as the first `[data-group-toggle] span[aria-hidden]`, which becomes a group caret, and the caret becomes a chevron. `row-withholds.browser.test.ts:654` checks the minimum target size of every `[data-group-toggle]` and no longer covers the 28 px section button. GREPPED and OPENED. Change to the plan: add these three lines to the re-read list at plan:80, with `mine-filter.browser.test.ts:114` opening `[data-section-toggle]` too.
