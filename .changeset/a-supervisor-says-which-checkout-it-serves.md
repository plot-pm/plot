`plot-fleetctl.sh --start` names the checkout a loaded supervisor serves when it refuses an already-loaded label: this repository, another checkout by path, or cannot determine. `--status` prints the same answer on a `serves:` line. The reading is the loaded job's `working directory` from `launchctl print gui/<uid>/<label>`, compared by physical path.
