`/plot-init` and `/plot-board-setup` propose the `Ticket prefixes` key, seeded from the prefix the probe measured and confirmed by a person. A measured prefix proposes a one-element list with its gap named — the probe reports the most frequent of the prefixes it counted, so scoping an inbox to it alone hides the work belonging to a repository's other tracker projects. No confirmed prefix writes no key: the inbox stays instance-wide, which is what every repository had before the key existed.
