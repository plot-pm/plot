The state gate decides in the domain: `plot-state-gate.sh` shrinks from 218 lines to a launcher, and `stateTransitionOf` and `stateTransitionRefusal` refuse a hand-written `State:` change unless a receipt, spent through the new `StateReceipts` port, clears it. `effectivePaths` decides which paths a commit carries and whether it takes the staged text or the working copy, so `git commit -am"msg"`, a chained `git add` of a glob or a directory, and a `..` pathspec from a subdirectory are read, and a staged `State:` change is refused although the working copy is reverted. A receipt is spent by a `rename`, so two concurrent gates clear one commit, not two.
