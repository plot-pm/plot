`plot-open-pr.sh` titles a slice PR `<plan title>: <wave heading>`, for example `Triggers replace the tick: Wave 1`. The title was the wave heading alone, so five PRs of two plans opened as `Wave 1` during sprint `triggers-and-connectors`. Where the plan has no title, or its title equals the wave heading, the title is the wave heading alone.
