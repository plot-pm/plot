`/plot-reconcile` reports the worktrees the fleet left behind, and a tree it cannot classify is named instead of skipped. The reaper recognised a desk by its `.plot-worker.pid` file or a legacy `plot-wt-` path and silently skipped anything else — not reaped, not kept, not counted, not named, which is how ten finished desks went unnoticed while it reported three. Such a tree now appears in the sweep with no removal command: the recognition test is unchanged, and only the silence goes. A hand-made checkout outside the configured worktree root still produces nothing.
