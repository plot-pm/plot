*One signal proposes, two signals ask* is a domain rule rather than a paragraph: `fromSignals` answers a set of structural signals with a proposal carrying its evidence, a question naming every present signal, or silence. A question carries no proposed word, so a caller cannot read half an answer, and `adoption.ts`'s `CI:` key reads that union instead of a flat string packing four answers into one field. The vendor names live in the collector, so a third CI system needs no edit to the rule.
