*One signal proposes, two signals ask* is a domain rule rather than a paragraph: `fromSignals` answers a set of structural signals with a proposal carrying its evidence, a question naming every present signal, or silence, and `proposeCi` applies it to the two CI signals. A question carries no proposed word, so a caller cannot read half an answer.
