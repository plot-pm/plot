A plan's card now shows what its slices cost. The rollup shipped with exactly one caller, in its own package, and `planSpendSummary` with none — so a plan's cost was computed correctly and no human being could see it, which is the third time that shape has shipped on this story. **The card leads with COVERAGE rather than the counters, and the reason is the eye rather than the arithmetic.** The rollup refuses to sum the four counters because cache reads measured 99.36% of a naive total, but that gates the arithmetic and gates nothing about what a reader does: over a real plan the counters span five orders of magnitude — `in 502 · out 107,182 · cache-write 528,331 · cache-read 40,690,450` — so the largest is 81,000× the smallest and a two-second reader takes it as the cost. Not summing is not the same as not being read as a sum. The badge therefore says `measured on 3 of 5 slices`, and the four counters ride the badge's tooltip, deferred rather than withheld, so refusing them at a glance is not refusing them. **The record is read ONCE per board build, hoisted above the per-plan loop**, which is where the plan's own `Done when` overrules its Design sentence: `planStatus` is called inside `for (const meta of metas)`, and `sliceSpendFile` caches the resolved directory rather than the contents, so a reading placed beside it is one `readFile` per plan per refresh — roughly 290 on this estate, on a board that refreshes every few seconds. `readPlanSpend` is deliberately not used for the same reason, since it re-reads per call by design; the shape that works is one `lines()` call feeding `planSpend`, a pure function, per plan. That is pinned by a counting stub asserting one call per build, because every returned value is byte-identical whether the record was read once or 290 times — verified by mutation, where a per-plan read fails exactly that one test and leaves the other twelve green. **A plan with nothing measured shows no cost rather than a zero**, since `reduce(…, 0)` over nothing is correct arithmetic and a lie; `absent` and `unreadable` ride as two counts and never one, the collapse this repo has shipped twice; and no fifth summed figure exists, pinned by a key-set assertion on the Card and a `.strict()` schema rather than by reading the DOM, where a summed field sitting unrendered in the payload would pass every assertion. The field is optional and follows the `rounds` precedent exactly — attached with `!== undefined` and never a truthiness test — so a payload carrying no cost renders precisely as it does today. Both render sites are covered structurally: `Board.tsx` and `Swimlanes.tsx` both render `PlanCard`.
