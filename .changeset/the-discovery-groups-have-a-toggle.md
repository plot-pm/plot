The Discovery tab puts a counted heading over each lane group (*Tickets (n)*, *Stories (n)*) and a *Tickets / Stories / Both* control above the lanes. The choice lives in `?show=tickets` or `?show=stories` and survives *Show archived*; *Both* writes no parameter. A heading counts every row of its group after the topic selection, and the tag cloud counts only the groups on screen.
