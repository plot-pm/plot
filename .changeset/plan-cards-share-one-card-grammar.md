Plan cards on the planning board and in swimlanes render through one shared card from a new domain value, `CardFacts`, in five rows: what it is, what it is part of, how far it is, what to do next, and who owns it since when. A disabled *Start work* says why on the card, a PR list of two or more collapses to one line that expands in place, and an unread fact reads as unknown in its own row instead of a chip of its own. The title is the card's only open link.
