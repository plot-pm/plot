A merged branch is no longer offered as claimable. `--offline` names the fetch and no longer silences the host on the offer path: `--next` and `--list-eligible` ask whether a branch's PR merged, and withhold a branch they could not ask about. Measured on the Plot estate — ten merged branches carried a ref whose tip commit is `plot: claim <branch>`, dated hours after their own merge, and one was re-claimed twice with four waves blocked behind it. Every other use of `--offline` still asks nothing. A repo with no remote reads `unasked` rather than `failed`, so it keeps offering work.
