The Delivery tab's `My sprint` toggle scopes the status card, You and Work items to the Active sprints the reader owns; with the toggle off they read every Active sprint and the status card shows the release cards. The toggle is disabled with the tooltip "You own no active sprint" where the reader owns none, and the chip formerly named "This sprint" is now "My sprint".
