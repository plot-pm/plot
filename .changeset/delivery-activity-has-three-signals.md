The Delivery tab shows activity as three signals. A running row or Fleet agent turns a spinner in a slot that stays reserved while idle, a changed row fades its accent once over 2 s, and the header's Fleet label turns a spinner while any agent works, whatever the Mine filter. A running agent's Tokens cell shows its context size (`143k ctx`).
