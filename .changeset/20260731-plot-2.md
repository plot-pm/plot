**Plot 2** — ceremony matched to the plan's weight, built from field
feedback. One coherent release across the manifesto, every spoke, the
companions, and the board.

**Ceremony is now a recorded choice, not a default.** Every plan answers
two questions once — `Review:` (pr, in-session, or ballot) and `Impl:`
(own branches, same branch, other repo, or none) — and `/plot-idea`
triages to the lightest path the repository allows. Repositories declare
their posture in `## Plot Config` (`Plan PRs`, `Implementation home`,
`Hosts plans`, `Tracker`, `Git host`); `Plan PRs: never` and
`Hosts plans: no` are hard gates. Undeclared repositories keep the
classic behavior.

**Approval is decoupled from implementation.** `/plot-approve` slims to
recording the approval through the declared channel; the new
`/plot-implement` starts or resumes the work (staleness preflight,
branch setup per the recorded answers, hand-off brief, `Started:`
records). The board splits Approved into Ready vs In progress, and a
PreToolUse phase gate (hooks/hooks.json) blocks implementation commits
on Draft plans — including the explicit `.plot/hold` review hold.

**Split-home and Bitbucket are first-class.** The Git-host adapter
`plot-host.sh` (gh/bb) makes every spoke host-neutral;
`plot-impl-status.sh` resolves cross-repo `owner/repo#N` references and
honors the `Plan directory` key and the remote default branch.

**Companions get the discipline layer.** story-tracking gains the triage
front door (umbrella rule: ticket → existing story → plan; overflow
signals; incident is a ticket type, not a trigger; late promotion with
guided backfill; ask-and-advise with human override), mirror-resistance
and one-home-for-session-narrative rules, richer statuses and template
blocks; new `plot-story-lint.sh` catches story-estate drift;
`/plot-sprint` declines where an external tracker owns sprints; the
"Plot in the Pipeline" doc draws the boundary to content-writing and
implementation skills.

**Validated before release**: an e2e choreography harness
(`pnpm test:e2e`, in CI) runs four lifecycle flows in sandbox repos, and
promptfoo eval suites run pre-merge caught and fixed two skill defects.
