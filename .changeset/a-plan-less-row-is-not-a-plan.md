A branch no plan names no longer renders as a nameless plan in NOT STARTED. A pushed branch with no plan and no pull request now goes to WAITING ON YOU as abandoned at any age, NOT STARTED draws no plan head for rows with no plan, and the section tally counts each such row as its own line. On Plot 2.20.0, two such branches rendered as `NOT STARTED (1 plan · 2 slices)` under a `PLAN (2)` head with no name.
