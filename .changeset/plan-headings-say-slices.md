A plan's branch section is `## Slices`, and CI refuses anything else in an unfinished plan. The parser still reads `## Branches` and `## Waves` and always will — a plan file may be a year old — but Plot no longer writes them. Three plans authored on 2026-09-05, all under the story that exists to fix this vocabulary, had drifted back to `## Branches` because nothing refused them.
