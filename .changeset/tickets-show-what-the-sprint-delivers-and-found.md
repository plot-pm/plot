The Delivery tab's Tickets section shows two groups under the tickets no plan names, each folded by default: Delivered by the sprint lists every ticket a scope plan's `Issue:` names, with its plan and phase, and Found during the sprint lists the review findings on the scope's PRs and the tickets its refuted verification claims name. Each row has a ⋯ with Create plan, Create story and Open on host; Create plan is disabled for a ticket a scope plan already names. A finding leaves the "no plan" rows and their hidden counts. The folded Tickets summary gains the `◎ N delivered by the sprint` and `⚑ N found` chips, with `?` while the tracker is unread.
