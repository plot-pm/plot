## Sprint Goal

Sprint `2026-W44-delivery-loop` delivered its 24 plans and release 2.30.0, but on 2026-11-03 the fleet needed four interventions, and fleetd merged no slice PR itself (its retrospective names each one). This sprint closes the gaps that keep the fleet from delivering alone, and proves it on its own scope.

- **Proof run:** #3061 (plan PR #3062) goes from approval to merged and delivered with no person. Every intervention it still needs is recorded in `## Notes`, not made silently.

- **Repair:** a release through `release-approve` leaves its issues at `plan:delivered` (#3066).
- **Done when:** release 2.30.1 waits for one approval, and the notes record zero interventions between each plan's approval and the release PR.
