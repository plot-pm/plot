## Sprint Goal

This sprint continues the story [the-fleet-delivers-a-scope-alone](../stories/the-fleet-delivers-a-scope-alone/STORY-the-fleet-delivers-a-scope-alone.md) and improves on sprint [`2026-W44-delivery-loop`](2026-W44-delivery-loop.md), whose goal it keeps. That sprint delivered all 24 plans and release 2.30.0 but did not meet its own goal: fleetd merged no slice PR (every merge was a person or an operator's script), and on 2026-11-03 alone the fleet needed four interventions. Its retrospective names each one.

- **Baseline (2.30.0):** every slice merge and four further interventions on the last day.
- **Target (2.30.1):** zero interventions between each plan's approval and the release PR, and one approval for the release.
- **Measurement:** every intervention a person or the master agent makes on this sprint's scope is recorded in `## Notes` with its date, its PR and the gap it shows, never made silently. #3061 (plan PR #3062) is the first plan measured.
- **The gaps the 2.30.0 retrospective found:** fleetd merges no slice PR while no Merger app is declared (#3063); a desk's design question waits for a person (#3064); the release PR needs a person for its review and its CI (#3065); a release through `release-approve` leaves its issues at `plan:delivered` (#3066).
- **Defects in the story's own plans:** the open plot-review findings on the 2.30.0 delivery-loop plans, triaged on 2026-11-03 (#3069).
