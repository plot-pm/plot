# Every Delivery section folds and says why it is empty

> Every section of the Delivery tab has a 28 px fold button and a header row that toggles, also when the section is empty, every jump opens its target section before it scrolls, and an open section with no rows shows a dashed frame with one sentence of kind clear, unread or stopped that names the scope and what the filter hides.

- **Type:** feature

- **Review:** pr
- **Impl:** own branches

## Changelog

- Every Delivery section folds: Release delivery, You, Work items, Fleet, Machine, Tickets and Not placed. Each has a 28 px fold button with a chevron that rotates on open, and a click anywhere else on the header row also toggles it. A click on a link, count or switch in the header row does only what that control does. Each reader's fold is kept in the browser.
- Every link that jumps into a section opens that section and its group first, then scrolls: the header's "open ›" links, the count, summary and phase-strip links, row anchors, the triage link and the "turn on ›" link to the fleet switches.
- An empty section still folds. Open, it shows a dashed frame with one sentence: green ✓ when nothing waits, amber ! when a reading failed, with the age of the last good reading where one exists, grey ■ when the source is stopped or cannot be read. Where the scope or the Mine filter hides rows, the sentence says so and gives the hidden count.
- An empty group inside an open section that holds rows shows its label, "— none" and its hint, so the section keeps its structure.

## Motivation

Plan 2 of 7 of the sprint "Delivery UI Polish". The approved design spec is `/Users/jwloka/Quatico/Agentic-Tools/plot/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-20-delivery-ui-polish-design.md` (outside this repository), with mockups in `/Users/jwloka/Quatico/Agentic-Tools/plot/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-20-delivery-ui-polish/`; `header-and-sections-v2.html` is the reference. The spec is canonical for everything this plan does not restate.

Today only some sections fold, and an empty section looks like a folded one or like a failed reading. The spec's anti-goal is direct: *"Could not read" that looks like "empty", or empty that looks like folded.* This plan implements goals 2 and 4, the shared section frame and the reachability rule. Plans 3 and 4 wait on its first slice; plan 1 can run in parallel.

## Design

### Approach

**Slice 1, the frame and the reachability rule:**

- **Reachability rule (first, because a fold changes what every other control can reach).** A jump to an anchor inside a folded section or group opens that section and group, persists the open state through `writeCollapsed` (`packages/board/src/app/lib/agent-rows/collapse.ts:82`), and then scrolls once the anchor is mounted. Today two mechanisms open a fold before a scroll: `jumpToReleasePlan` (`packages/board/src/app/components/AgentList.tsx:527-535`) and `expandSection` (`AgentList.tsx:520-524`), which `BlockedByMark`'s jump calls with the legacy group word `'done'` (`packages/board/src/app/lib/agent-rows/marks.tsx:325-355`). `scrollToAnchor` (`packages/board/src/app/components/DeliveryRows.tsx:20-28`) returns without effect for an anchor that is not mounted. This slice replaces both with one jump that every control uses: the You count links (`AgentList.tsx:1575`), the phase-strip links (`:1588`), the summary links (`:1605`), the held-switch "turn on ›" link to `#fleet-controls` (`:1738`), `BlockedByMark`'s jump, the agent link lines' own `scrollTo` (`AgentList.tsx:1253-1256`), App's reveal effect that scrolls to `agent-row-<branch>` (`packages/board/src/app/App.tsx:1273-1283`), `FleetCard`'s "Fleet controls ›" (`onJump('fleet-controls')`, `packages/board/src/app/components/FleetCard.tsx:64`) until plan 4 removes `FleetCard`, the footer's "details" button (below), the TodoList jumps until plan 4 removes `TodoList`, plan 3's summary chips, and plan 4's "open ›" and triage links. Row anchor links call the module-level `scrollToAnchor` directly today (`DeliveryRows.tsx:47`); they take the jump through a new `onJump` field of `LinkHandlers` (`DeliveryRows.tsx:7-12`).
- **The anchor-to-section mapping is a view property.** `anchorOwner(anchor, view)` in `delivery-view.ts` (`packages/board/src/app/lib/agent-rows/delivery-view.ts`) returns `{ section, group | null, hidden: 'filter' | 'inner-fold' | null }`, or `null` for an anchor no section owns. It covers new section anchors `delivery-section-<key>` (sections carry no id today, `AgentList.tsx:1636`), group anchors (`groupAnchor`, `delivery-view.ts:277`), item anchors (`itemAnchor`, `delivery-view.ts:384`, resolved through the view's placement), agent rows `agent-row-<branch>` (`packages/board/src/app/lib/agent-rows/working-agents.ts:218`), the slice rows `BlockedByMark` selects (`[data-slice-row][data-slice-plan]`, `marks.tsx:344-345`), `#tickets` (`TICKETS_ANCHOR`, imported from `@plot-pm/domain` at `packages/board/src/app/components/TicketsSection.tsx:2` and used at `:55`), `#fleet-controls` (`AgentList.tsx:1623`) and Not placed's existing id `unplaced-details` (`AgentList.tsx:1769`). A unit test covers each section and each anchor kind; a `null` answer scrolls only.
- **A jump whose anchor stays hidden.** `hidden: 'filter'` answers for an item that the whole readings hold and the shown placement does not, because the scope or Mine hides it; the link then shows disabled with the reason "hidden by Mine" or "hidden by the scope" (no hidden UI), and a unit test covers that case. `hidden: 'inner-fold'` answers for a Tickets row behind `… n more · show` (`foldLabel`, `packages/domain/src/rules/tickets-section.ts:41`); the jump then opens that inner fold too. Where the anchor is still unmounted after the open, the jump scrolls to the section anchor `delivery-section-<key>`, so a jump never ends without a visible effect.
- **The shared frame.** Every section header row holds a 28 px fold button (chevron rotates on open), the section title and the summary slot, on one line. The 28 px button is the one element with `aria-expanded` and the accessible name. The row click is a pointer shortcut and carries no `role="button"`; this replaces the `role="button"` wrapper `releaseFold` puts around interactive children today (`AgentList.tsx:1514-1528`). A section has three states: folded (one line), open (groups and rows), and open-but-empty (dashed frame, one sentence).
- **Header controls stop propagation.** Every control inside a toggling header row stops click and key propagation, as `ReleaseHeaderCounts` does (`packages/board/src/app/components/ReleasePlanParts.tsx:73-77`): the You count and phase-strip links, the summary links, and the Fleet switches and stepper (`AgentList.tsx:1622-1629`). A browser test proves that a click on a header switch or count leaves `aria-expanded` unchanged.
- **One fold rule, as a view property.** `isFoldable(level, rowCount)` and `isFolded(level, key, rowCount, collapsed, always)` in `collapse.ts`, written as arrow functions beside today's declarations (`readCollapsed`, `collapse.ts:56`), replace the inline decisions in `fold` and `releaseFold` (`AgentList.tsx:1480-1532`); both call them. Unit tests: an empty section is foldable; an empty group is not; a stored key folds an empty section (today `isFolded` returns false for it, `AgentList.tsx:1481`); the Fleet `working` and `blocked` groups never fold (`fleetOpen`, `AgentList.tsx:1713`).
- **The `isCollapsible` reversal, for sections only.** `isCollapsible(rowCount) => rowCount > 0` (`collapse.ts:107`) makes an empty group unfoldable, because folding would hide the group's hint. For **sections** that is replaced: every section folds, also when empty, and the folded header carries the explanation the hint carried (plan 3 draws it as the summary; this plan carries the empty kind's mark there until then). **Groups** inside an open section never fold when empty. Update the comment above `isCollapsible` (`collapse.ts:93-106`) and its test (`packages/board/test/unit/agent-list.test.ts:1410`, *"isCollapsible — an empty group hides nothing"*) to describe that baseline.
- **A folded section renders its header line only.** Today `WorkingCounts` and `FleetAlert` (`AgentList.tsx:1701-1706`) and `MachineSection` (`:1710`) render with no fold guard. They become fold content: `WorkingCounts` and `MachineSection` render only while open. A `FleetAlert` at prominence `alert` stays visible while Fleet is folded, in the summary slot, because that sentence is the one a person missed for an hour (`packages/board/src/app/components/FleetControls.tsx:400-403`). A browser test proves that a folded Machine section renders no `MachineSection`.
- **Seven sections.** The five of `DELIVERY_LAYOUT` (`packages/domain/src/rules/delivery-placement.ts:12-18`: `release-delivery`, `you`, `work-items`, `fleet`, `machine`) and the two the tab renders after them: *Not placed — agent branches* (`AgentList.tsx:1768`) and Tickets (`TicketsSectionView`, rendered at `AgentList.tsx:1792`, anchor `#tickets`). Tickets keeps its `#tickets` anchor on the `<section>`, so the anchor survives a fold.
- **Not placed has one control.** Today the section renders only when `view.notPlaced.length > 0 && showUnplaced` (`AgentList.tsx:1768`), where `showUnplaced` is session state (`:684`) that the footer's "details" button toggles (`:1858-1869`, `data-unplaced-toggle`). The section fold becomes its one control: `showUnplaced` is removed, the footer's "details" becomes a jump that opens the section through the reachability rule, the section takes the title "Not placed — agent branches" and the key `not-placed`, and it starts folded, so its default matches today's hidden one. It renders only when it has rows. The `<section>` keeps `id="unplaced-details"` and `data-unplaced` (`AgentList.tsx:1769`), so the anchor and the test selector survive.
- **Fold state per reader.** Folds persist in `localStorage` through `collapse.ts` (`readCollapsed`, `writeCollapsed`, `collapse.ts:56,82`). `KNOWN_KEYS` (`collapse.ts:6`) derives from `DELIVERY_LAYOUT`, which holds no Tickets or Not placed section; `KNOWN_KEYS` becomes the layout's keys plus a list `EXTRA_SECTION_KEYS = ['tickets', 'not-placed']` in `collapse.ts`, and plan 7 adds its two group keys to that list. `COLLAPSED_BY_DEFAULT` (`collapse.ts:17`) becomes `['free', 'not-placed']`, so every other section starts open.
- **A new default reaches a stored set once.** `readCollapsed` applies `COLLAPSED_BY_DEFAULT` only when nothing is stored or no stored key is known (`collapse.ts:63-74`), so a reader with a stored `["free"]` would see Not placed open after this change. A second storage key records the default keys already applied to this reader; on read, each key of `COLLAPSED_BY_DEFAULT` not in that record joins the set once, and the record is written. A reader who then opens the section keeps it open. Unit tests: a stored `["free"]` reads `free` and `not-placed`; a stored `["free"]` with `not-placed` already in the record reads `free` only; no stored set reads the default.
- **Section toggles take their own attribute.** A section toggle carries `data-section-toggle="<key>"`; groups keep `data-group-toggle`, so group selectors in tests stay unambiguous.
- **Reduced motion.** The chevron's rotation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative: it switches without animation.

**Slice 2, the empty kinds:**

- **Three empty kinds, as one view property.** `sectionEmptyKind(input): SectionEmpty | null` beside `delivery-view.ts` returns `null` for a section with rows, and otherwise `{ kind: 'clear' | 'unread' | 'stopped', sentence, note | null, hidden | null }`. The three kinds are clear (green ✓, nothing to do), unread (amber !, a reading failed) and stopped (grey ■, a source that does not run or cannot be read). It absorbs the one empty-kind decision a `.tsx` file makes today (`TicketsSection.tsx:82`). One unit test per table row, plus the non-empty case:

  | Section | Input | Empty when | Kind and sentence |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Release delivery | `view.releasePlanCount` | 0 | clear: "No approved plan belongs to a release or sprint yet." |
  | You | `section.count` (`delivery-view.ts:617`), `fleet.issueAnswer` | 0 | clear: "Nothing waits on you."; unread when `issueAnswer` is `failed` |
  | Work items | `section.count` | 0 | clear: "No work item is at a gate, being built or queued." |
  | Fleet | `supervisorVerdict` (`packages/domain/src/rules/supervisor-reading.ts:388`) over `fleet.supervisor`, agent count | no agent rows | `up` with 0 agents: clear "No agent runs."; `down`: stopped "The fleet is not running."; `died`: stopped with the alert's detail; `unknown`: stopped with the supervisor's `note` wording, never clear |
  | Machine | `machineHealth` (`packages/domain/src/rules/machine-health.ts:216`) headroom | headroom `unmeasured` | stopped, with `machineHealth`'s own word "not measured" (`machine-health.ts:92`) |
  | Tickets | `ticketsSection` state (`packages/domain/src/rules/tickets-section.ts:32,57`) | state `rows` with 0 rows | clear: "No ticket waits for a plan."; `failed`: unread; `unsupported`: stopped with `issueAbsence`; `unasked`: unread with "The tracker has not been read yet." and no failure |
  | Not placed | `view.notPlaced` | never: it renders only with rows | — |

  `SupervisorState` is `'up' | 'down' | 'unknown' | 'died'` (`supervisor-reading.ts:58`); the mapping reads `supervisorVerdict` and adds no field. Machine adds no second "no reading" vocabulary.
- **Retry is dropped; an unread frame shows the last good reading** (operator decision, 2026-11-20). No Retry control exists in `packages/board/src/app`, and no route re-asks the tracker. An unread frame shows the failure through `issueNote` (`packages/board/src/app/lib/agent-rows/host-notes.ts:419`), which appends the age of the last good rows from `issueAgeSeconds` (`packages/board/src/contract/schema.ts:4895`, set at `packages/board/src/server/fleet.ts:9040` from `entry.issueAt`). Where `issueAgeSeconds` is `null`, no earlier reading exists in the payload, and the frame shows the failure (`issueError`) alone.
- **Empty versus hidden: the sentence names the scope and the hidden count.** Where the scope or the Mine filter hides rows, the sentence names the scope and the count that the filter hides ("Nothing waits on you in my sprints · 3 hidden by Mine"), and the frame shows a button that calls the setter the Mine chip or the scope toggle already calls, so the frame adds no second filter state. The scope adds its name to the sentence and no count of its own, because no function computes a scope-hidden count today. Release delivery reads `deliveryMapping({ ...input, mode: 'all' })` (`releaseCardsOf`, `packages/board/src/app/lib/agent-rows/delivery-readings.ts:571`; `releaseCards`, `packages/domain/src/rules/release-card.ts:117`), so only its scope varies.
- **`sectionEmptyKind` decides no ownership.** It takes the hidden count as an input, and the count comes from the same rows the section shows, so the sentence and the rows cannot disagree whichever Mine rule the filters apply. For items (You, Work items), the count is the items of `deliveryMapping`'s `wholeReadings` (`delivery-readings.ts:471`) that the whole placement puts in the section, minus the items shown there; `mineHiddenCount` (`packages/domain/src/rules/mine-mode.ts:95`) cannot serve, because it counts rows by ownership before placement and cannot say which section a hidden row would sit in. For Fleet, the count is `fleet.agents` minus the agents `agentsForReader` keeps (`packages/board/src/app/lib/agent-rows/mine-filter.ts:236`, called at `AgentList.tsx:644`); `workersHiddenByFilter` (`AgentList.tsx:879`) counts the sprint filter, not Mine, and stays as it is. For Tickets, the count is `ticketsSection`'s `hidden` input behind *show all* (`tickets-section.ts:65-73`).
- **Two Mine vocabularies.** The board's Mine mode is `MineMode` (`'all' | 'mine-and-unknown' | 'mine'`, `mine-mode.ts:12`), and placement reads `DeliveryMine` (`'off' | 'mine' | 'strict'`, `packages/domain/src/rules/delivery-placement.ts:32`) through the map `MINE` (`delivery-readings.ts:143`: `all → off`, `mine-and-unknown → mine`, `mine → strict`). This plan names the `DeliveryMine` words, as the spec does.
- **Mine, per section.** Mine applies to You, Work items, Fleet and Tickets. The rule itself (operator decision, 2026-11-20: `mine` shows a row assigned to the reader or whose plan is the reader's, plus rows of unknown owner; `strict` keeps today's rule) is built by plan 5's first slice, `feature/mine-means-mine-or-my-plan`, in the filters; this plan reads its output only. Unit test cells, for each of You, Work items and Tickets: `release` × {off, mine, strict} and `my-sprint` × {off, mine, strict}, as fixture counts (0 shown and n hidden, and 0 shown and 0 hidden), each asserting the empty kind, the sentence and the hidden count. Release delivery has the two scope cells only.
- **Fleet and Machine take no scope.** The spec shows the whole machine in both scopes (spec *Header*, "Fleet and Machine show the whole machine in both scopes"), so their sentence never names the scope, and Fleet's `my-sprint` cells equal its `release` cells. Mine still applies to Fleet. Machine takes neither.
- **My sprint with several sprints** (operator decision, 2026-11-20). Where `deliveryScope` returns several sprints (`delivery-scope.ts:56-65`), the sections combine all of them, and a scope sentence names them ("in my sprints · <a>, <b>").
- **Frame or content.** A section shows the frame where `sectionEmptyKind` answers non-null, and otherwise its content: a measured Machine section has no rows and no groups (`DELIVERY_LAYOUT`, `delivery-placement.ts:17`, `groups: []`) and still shows its meters, and a unit test asserts that it answers `null`. A section with content shows every group, and an empty group shows its label, "— none" and its hint. Today `AgentList.tsx:1712` filters `group.count > 0`, so an empty group does not render; the hints already exist in `GROUP_LABELS` (`delivery-view.ts:66-76`) and reach the view as `GroupView.hint` (`delivery-view.ts:252`).
- **Tickets keeps its absence selector.** The Tickets frame of kind unread or stopped carries `data-tickets-absent`, which `TicketsSection.tsx:80-83` renders today, so `tickets-section.browser.test.ts:210-220` keeps its selector.
- **Fleet keeps its switches when empty.** The switches stay where they render today, in the Fleet section heading (`AgentList.tsx:1622-1630`), also while the section is open and empty. Plan 5's `feature/fleet-switches-in-fleet-section` owns the switch row at the top of the open section and its place above the stopped frame.
